- [Vast](https://vast.ai/)
  They have an assorted GPUs and RAM sizes, the cheapest I found that would be
  interesting for me was an RTX 3090 24 GB VRAM at $0.11/hr, but that gives
  roughly the same price I'm currently paying ($20) for Anthropic models (aka
  the ones Claude Code uses) for 8 hours a day, only 5 days a week. So there's
  no point in paying those numbers, cause it's not cheaper and definitely the
  model I would run there would be worst than the ones I can access through
  Claude Code. 

  The globally cheapest was an GTX 1660 S 6 GB VRAM at $0.02/hr, but I have an
  RTX 3050 with 4 GB VRAM, there's no big difference.

  **Conclusion:** still not worth it.
